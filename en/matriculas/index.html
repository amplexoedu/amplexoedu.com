The club

Many ways to learn. One community to belong to.

Clube Amplexo Educação is an online academic enrichment club for students from preschool through 9th grade.

One enrollment gives access to the activities suited to the student's stage and preparation, within the club's schedule. Families don't need to sign up for each course separately.

Having access to everything doesn't mean having to do everything.

Variety widens the choices. Each family plans participation around the child's interests, availability and current stage.

Making the most of the club isn't about a packed schedule: it's about finding what makes sense right now.

What Amplexo brings together

  • Different learning paths
  • Strengthening the basics and opportunities to go deeper
  • Preparation for academic challenges and olympiads
  • Arts, language, science, technology and personal growth
  • Academic follow-up
  • Community, connection and support for families

For kids who love asking questions

Amplexo is for children and teens who love to ask, investigate, create, read, experiment and solve challenges.

We have experience with neurodivergent students, including gifted and talented students, but the club is not only for them. No diagnosis, Mensa membership or previous medals are required to join.

How it works

Tracks and challenges organized by stage, knowledge and group interests.

From the enrollment form to follow-up throughout the year, every step starts by listening to the family.

  1. 1

    The family shares

    In the enrollment form, the family tells us about the student's profile and interests and picks the activities they are most interested in, with no minimum or maximum.

  2. 2

    Coordinators get to know each profile

    The coordinators review the students' profiles and interests.

  3. 3

    Groups take shape

    Groups and activities are organized by age, school grade, knowledge and interests, suited to each student's stage and preparation.

  4. 4

    Progress is followed closely

    Progress is tracked through reports, practice tests, meetings and conversations with the coordinators.

Good to know

  • Advanced courses may include an assessment of the required foundation.
  • If a student is beyond the planned content, the coordinators consider placing them in more advanced classes.
  • Choosing an activity doesn't guarantee automatic access to any class. Participation depends on fit with the group, the schedule and the routine, and there may be time conflicts.

Does my child need to take part in every activity?

No. Having access to everything doesn't mean having to do everything. The family picks the activities they're most interested in, with no minimum or maximum, and plans participation around the child's availability and current stage.

Does my child need to be a Mensa member or gifted?

No. We have experience with neurodivergent students, including gifted and talented students, but the club is not only for them. No diagnosis, Mensa membership or previous medals are required.

How are groups formed?

Based on the information in the enrollment form, the coordinators consider age, school grade, knowledge and interests. Advanced courses may include an assessment of the required foundation. If a student is beyond the planned content, the coordinators consider placing them in more advanced classes.

Choosing an activity doesn't guarantee automatic access to any class, and there may be time conflicts.

Are classes online? Are they recorded?

Classes are online and live, and they are not recorded. For studying between classes, the Amplexo Educação platform offers its own courses and content.

Is there academic follow-up?

Yes. Students' progress is tracked through reports, practice tests, meetings and conversations with the coordinators.

Are all olympiad registrations included?

No. For 2027, the Medalhei Olympiads and the Singapore Math Olympiad Preliminary Round have no extra cost, and other olympiads have discounted registration. Some opportunities may be paid separately.

To use these benefits, the student must be active and in good standing with the club in the month of the exam.

Where can I find fees and terms?

Fees and terms are presented when you get in touch, so our team can get to know your family and go over each point calmly. Just message us on WhatsApp.
