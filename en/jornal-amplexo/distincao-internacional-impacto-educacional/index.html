International recognition

Clube Amplexo Educação receives international distinction for educational impact

The Brazilian organization was recognized in the EDU FOR GOOD – Distinction category of the Brands For Good Awards 2026 for its commitment to purpose-driven education, human development and positive impact.

By the Amplexo Educação TeamJuly 30, 2026 · Jornal Amplexo

Clube Amplexo Educação has received the EDU FOR GOOD – Distinction international recognition, awarded by the Brands For Good Awards 2026 in Singapore.

The awards recognize organizations that embed purpose in their practices and turn their values into actions capable of generating social, environmental or inclusive impact. The EDU FOR GOOD category highlights initiatives that use education to make learning more accessible and transformative, strengthen communities and inspire lasting social change.

According to the official announcement, the 2026 edition brought together more than 110 brands from over 25 countries. The Distinction rating recognizes organizations that have demonstrated significant impact, innovation, effectiveness and alignment with responsible practices.

“Receiving this distinction is not just an institutional achievement. It recognizes a story built by students, families, teachers, partners and professionals who believe that education can transform lives. We want our students to achieve academic results, but above all, we want them to understand everything they can do for the world with what they learn,” says Clarissa Vergara.

The recognition also marks a new chapter in Amplexo’s institutional work, as the organization plans to expand initiatives focused on educating for good, student agency and projects capable of turning knowledge into social impact.

For the organization, educating for good means not letting any talent go unseen, creating real opportunities and mobilizing families, educators, specialists, companies and partners so that every child is recognized and valued.

Special attention goes to children and young people who still face barriers to fully developing their potential, especially neurodivergent students, who have unique ways of thinking, learning and understanding the world. When they find a welcoming environment, encouragement, the right support and real opportunities, they can turn their ideas and talents into meaningful initiatives capable of making a positive impact on society.

“We are turning this recognition into an even more concrete educational commitment. We want to expand our support for projects born from our students’ ideas, strengthen purpose-driven initiatives and look intentionally at the impact education can have beyond the classroom. More than celebrating an achievement, we want to make it a starting point for new opportunities, consistent action and real transformation in the lives of people and communities,” adds Clarissa.

About the Brands For Good Awards

The Brands For Good Awards recognize values- and purpose-driven brands and leaders. The awards include the Business for Good, Tech for Good, Culture for Good, Leadership for Good and Edu for Good categories. Organizations go through an eligibility screening and an evaluation by an independent panel, which considers purpose, positive impact, innovation and commitment to responsible growth.

About Clube Amplexo Educação

Clube Amplexo Educação is a curriculum enrichment club dedicated to the cognitive, academic, emotional and social development of children and young people. The institution develops educational projects that value curiosity, student agency and different ways of learning, always with close attention to inclusion.

Its methodology combines live classes, proprietary materials, hands-on projects, ongoing pedagogical support and guidance for families, valuing both academic achievement and the development of students’ autonomy, curiosity and social responsibility.
