Amplexo Calendar

Calendar brings together science, art and technology

By the Amplexo Educação TeamJuly 25, 2026 · Jornal Amplexo

August arrives with plenty of news at Clube Amplexo Educação. The new calendar brings together experiences in science, art, technology, mathematics, language, culture, sports and citizenship, opening up different paths so that every child can learn, investigate and develop their interests.

Between labs, interdisciplinary projects and olympiad training sessions, students will have opportunities to experiment, create, research and turn knowledge into action. Topics include marine biology, robotics, theater, music, chess, environmental awareness, news writing and much more.

More than offering lots of activities, our goal is to build meaningful learning journeys. Each family will be able to choose, with peace of mind, the experiences that make sense for their child, respecting their interests, where they are in their learning and their daily routine.

August will be a month of new discoveries, projects and gatherings. A calendar designed to spark curiosity and show that learning can also be light, enjoyable and full of purpose.

Amplexo Educação — Knowledge that embraces. Action that transforms.
