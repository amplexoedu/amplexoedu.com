Purpose

Clube Amplexo

Clube Amplexo is more than an educational initiative: it is a movement for transformation. We spark a desire for high-level learning, encourage students to overcome challenges, and give them space to express their ideas, always respecting each person's history, individuality, and pace.

01

True inclusion

Our community welcomes neurotypical and neurodivergent children and teens with a wide range of interests, talents, and needs. We support curious students who are looking for deeper learning, new challenges, or supplementary instruction, offering a listening ear, guidance, and opportunities so that each of them can fully develop their potential.

02

Brilliant minds, willing hearts

For us, inclusion and social responsibility mean recognizing what makes each person unique, opening up new paths, and giving students a voice. That is why academic excellence goes hand in hand with empathy and a commitment to doing good, because shaping brilliant minds only makes sense when we also shape hearts willing to build a better world.

01

Classes, projects, and mentoring

Along with live and recorded classes and high-quality materials, we keep listening closely to students and families to understand their needs and foster their physical, emotional, and cognitive well-being.

We take part in initiatives such as the Blue School Program and in social projects, and we encourage experiences in sports, art, culture, science, technology, innovation, and student leadership.
