Purpose

Embrace and be embraced by Education

Clube Amplexo is more than an educational initiative: it is a movement for transformation. We spark the desire for high-level learning, encourage students to overcome challenges and give them room to express their ideas, always respecting each person's story, individuality and pace.

Our community welcomes neurotypical and neurodivergent children and teens with different interests, talents and needs. We support curious students looking for deeper learning, new challenges or extra academic support, offering a listening ear, encouragement and opportunities so that each of them can fully develop their potential.

For us, inclusion and social responsibility mean recognizing what makes each person unique, opening up new paths and giving students a voice. That is why academic excellence goes hand in hand with empathy and a commitment to doing good: shaping brilliant minds only makes sense when we also shape hearts ready to build a better world.

Online academic enrichment

Learning environments

01

Classes, projects and mentoring

Along with live and recorded classes and high-quality materials, we keep listening closely to students and families to understand their needs and support their physical, emotional and cognitive health.

We take part in initiatives such as the Blue School Program (Escola Azul) and in social projects. We also encourage and support experiences in sports, art, culture, science, technology, innovation and student leadership, building a broad, welcoming education connected to each student's interests.

02

A varied calendar

Knowledge in motion, with guidance and meaningful challenges.

We offer a rich, ongoing program that broadens the academic experience and opens up different paths for growth. Art, music, the newspaper, olympiads, science lab, theater and technology are all part of this journey. Even online, we nurture closeness, care and affection, so that every meeting is a true embrace in the form of education.

03

Partnerships and platforms

In addition to our own platform, we give students access to tools such as Medalhei, Educacross, SuperAutor and QuBits, along with partnerships with institutions such as Instituto Azlan, Sócios Mirins AEITA, Mensa Brasil and SMETA Olympiad. This network broadens students' contact with technology, innovation, connections and new opportunities for learning, growth and leadership.

04

Olympiad Journey

We prepare and support each student through every stage of their olympiad journey. We celebrate far beyond medals: every challenge faced, every lesson learned and every step taken is an achievement. We are a national and international reference in olympiad awards, combining academic excellence, strategy, cheering and care.

We welcome students from the very first step. We prepare them for the greatest challenges.

High performance grows from a solid foundation. From beginner to advanced levels, students find the support, tools and challenges to go further and further.
