✦ THE PROGRAM

Embrace and be embraced by education.

We want to raise a generation that recognizes real problems, creates responsible solutions and measures the impact of its actions with awareness, creativity and responsibility.

Projects that Embrace

Students develop initiatives that address social, community and environmental challenges, turning ideas into concrete actions for the common good. Families are invited to support, celebrate and share every project.

Rather than picking a single standout, the program's purpose is to recognize the importance of every action, welcome every proposal and lovingly follow the growth of each initiative.

✦ HOW IT WORKS

From idea to impact: a path of support and recognition.

01

The student creates

They identify a real need and turn their idea into a project with intention and purpose.

02

The project is presented

The initiative enters the program's selection process, with clearly defined goals, audience, actions and needs.

03

The committee chooses

Every month, one outstanding initiative is selected to receive strategic support and close mentorship.

04

The impact takes center stage

When well executed, the project joins the Map of Good and may receive the Láurea Global Embrace.

✦ RECOGNITION

Láurea Global Embrace

Celebrating real impact.

The Láurea Global Embrace is Amplexo's highest honor for outstanding initiatives, recognizing student projects that make a real impact on society.

The honor will be awarded to initiatives that demonstrate consistent development, autonomy, proactivity and a commitment to the common good. To receive the award, the project must follow a clear impact framework and present concrete evidence of the results achieved and the changes it has brought about.

“Receiving this honor is proof that an idea can leave the drawing board and transform lives. It shows that knowledge, courage, empathy and action can become a solution that truly makes a difference.”
Láurea Global Embrace

Recognition criteria

  • Relevance of the problem identified
  • Clarity of goals
  • Planning and organization
  • Student autonomy and leadership
  • Creativity of the solution
  • Community involvement
  • Quality of execution
  • Evidence presented
  • Results achieved
  • Potential for continuity
  • Commitment to the common good
  • Transparency in presenting data
